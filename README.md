# From Gurukul to Generative AI: Re-envisioning Indian Education

## About the Research

This research explores how Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), ethical technology, and digital learning can contribute to the transformation of Indian education towards Viksit Bharat @2047.

The study examines the role of digital platforms in improving access to IKS, supporting cultural relevance and value-oriented learning, and strengthening collaborative and integrated learning.

## Research Objectives

- To examine how digital platforms support access to and learning of Indian Knowledge Systems.
- To assess perceptions of the cultural relevance and value-oriented learning benefits of digitally enabled IKS.
- To examine perceptions of collaborative and integrated digital learning related to IKS.
- To propose an Ethical–IKS–AI education framework aligned with NEP 2020 and Viksit Bharat @2047.

## Research Methodology

The study follows a descriptive research design based on a ten-item Likert-scale questionnaire.

The questionnaire covers:

- Digital Accessibility & Learning
- Cultural Relevance & Value Education
- Collaborative & Integrated Learning
- Overall IKS Integration

Responses are measured using a five-point Likert scale ranging from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.

## Data Analysis

The working analysis uses a **240-record simulated/mock dataset** for demonstrating the descriptive analytical framework. These records are not actual participant responses.

### Key Results

| Dimension | Mean |
|---|---:|
| Digital Accessibility & Learning | 3.935 |
| Cultural Relevance & Value Education | 3.908 |
| Collaborative & Integrated Learning | 3.833 |
| Overall IKS Integration | 3.901 |

The results indicate generally positive perceptions of digitally enabled IKS learning within the simulated dataset.

## Key Findings

- Self-paced IKS learning recorded the highest individual item mean.
- Respondents generally viewed digital technology as a useful bridge between traditional Indian knowledge and contemporary education.
- Cultural relevance and value-oriented learning showed positive perceptions.
- Online IKS communities recorded the comparatively lowest item mean, suggesting scope for stronger structured collaborative learning.
- The study does not claim causal relationships between Ethical AI, Generative AI, IKS integration, and sustainability outcomes.

## Proposed Framework

The research proposes an:

**Ethical–IKS–AI Education Model**

> Ethical AI + Generative AI + Indian Knowledge Systems  
> → Ethical, Sustainable & Value-Based Education  
> → Viksit Bharat @2047

The framework positions ethical principles as a governance layer for responsible technology-enabled education.

## Limitations

- The current dataset is simulated and cannot substitute for genuine field responses.
- The questionnaire primarily measures perceptions of digitally enabled IKS learning.
- Ethical AI, Generative AI usage, and sustainability outcomes were not directly measured by the ten-item instrument.
- The descriptive research design does not support causal inference.

## Future Research

Future studies can collect verified primary data and use validated measures for:

- Ethical AI
- Generative AI usage
- Sustainability outcomes
- Data privacy
- Fairness and transparency
- Social responsibility

Appropriate reliability, correlation, regression, or structural-equation techniques can then be applied where supported by the measurement model.
